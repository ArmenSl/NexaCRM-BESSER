NexaCRM
AI-Powered Intelligent CRM
Vision
A CRM that works for you. NexaCRM automates time-consuming tasks, enriches your data in real-time, and guides you toward the highest-potential opportunities using artificial intelligence.
Problem Statement
Sales teams spend more time entering data and searching for information than actually selling. Traditional CRMs are passive databases that require constant updating discipline and offer no actionable intelligence.
Features
1.	Contact & Company Management: Create, edit, delete, and view contact and company records. Advanced search and filtering capabilities.
2.	Automatic Enrichment: From a LinkedIn URL, automatically retrieve contact information (name, job title, company, profile picture).
3.	AI Lead Scoring: Automatically calculated sales potential score for each contact, dynamically updated based on interactions.
4.	Writing Assistant: Generate personalized prospecting or follow-up emails, taking into account the contact's context and interaction history.
5.	Sales Pipeline: Dashboard view of ongoing opportunities with filtering options by score, status, or assigned salesperson.
6.	Interaction History: Chronological timeline of all exchanges (emails, notes, calls) associated with each contact.
Success Criteria
Cross-cutting Prerequisites:
— Application deployed and accessible via URL
— Functional authentication (sign up, login, logout)
Per Feature:
1.	A user can create, edit, delete, and search contacts and companies
2.	Enrichment via LinkedIn URL returns at minimum: name, current job title, company, photo
3.	Each contact displays a lead score that updates automatically
4.	An email generated from a contact record mentions the name, company, and relationship context
5.	The dashboard displays opportunities with functional filters (score, status, assignment)
6.	Interaction history is visible and chronological on each contact record
